The everyday flow

Open. Fix. Done.

01

Open

Double-click a photo, or start Lucid Viewer to see your recent and pinned photos. The next photo in the folder is already loading while you look at this one.

02

Make it right

Exposure, colors, crop and straighten in the side panel. Draw, sign, rate with 1–5, compare before and after with a draggable divider.

03

Save, share or convert

Save, export with a size estimate, share with Windows, or drag the file out. Every step can be undone, even a delete.

Measured, not promised

Fast where you feel it.

Time from starting the program until the photo is on screen. Lucid Viewer keeps the libjpeg-turbo and AVX2 pipeline of JPEGView and shows a fast first frame of large photos; everything the first frame does not need is loaded later.

How this was measured

Warm start, median of 10 runs (6 for Windows Photos and ImageGlass) on an AMD Ryzen 7 7840HS with 16 GB RAM and an NVMe SSD, Windows 11 Pro build 26300, 2026-11-05. The test images are generated by the benchmark script; it detects the moment a magenta marker in the image appears in the new window. With small photos the original JPEGView is about 20 ms faster: it draws no toolbar, side panel or filmstrip. Script and raw results are in the repository.

The toolbox

One window. Everything you reach for.

The things you used to open a second program for.

01

Start screen

Recently opened and pinned photos with thumbnails, instead of an empty window. Ctrl+B pins, Alt+Home returns.

02

Edit

Exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature, tint, sharpness, vignette and local contrast, with an Auto button.

03

Before / after

Drag a divider across the photo to compare the original and the edit (Ctrl+K).

04

Crop & straighten

Aspect ratios, free rotation, perspective correction, resize and lossless JPEG rotation.

05

Draw & sign

Pen, highlighter, rectangle, arrow, text and pixelate. Save your signature once, place, move and resize it on any document.

06

Remove background

An AI model on your PC cuts out the subject. Save it as a transparent PNG. Nothing is uploaded.

07

Copy text (OCR)

Every word is framed, select with the mouse and copy. Uses the text recognition built into Windows.

08

Screenshot tool

PrtScn freezes the screen. Select a region or window, annotate, pixelate, read its text, copy or save. A magnifier shows each pixel's color.

09

Convert anywhere

Right-click in Explorer → Convert to JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, PDF and more, resized or without metadata. Also for whole folders and from the command line.

10

Star ratings

Rate with 0–5, stored in the file so Explorer sees it too. Sort and filter the folder by stars.

11

Metadata

All metadata of the file, grouped. Edit titles, dates and tags, copy everything, or remove the GPS location in one click.

12

Similar photos

Find duplicates and near-duplicates in a folder, side by side.

13

Export

Format, quality and size with an exact file size before you save.

14

Undo everything

Ctrl+Z works for edits, drawings, crops, ratings, metadata changes and even moving a photo to the Recycle Bin.

15

Make it yours

A searchable settings window, a shortcut editor where you just press the new key, dark and light theme.

Privacy

Viewing, editing, OCR and background removal run on your PC. Lucid Viewer has no analytics, no account and no cloud. It only goes online when you ask it to download the background removal model.

Open source

GPL v2, built in the open on GitHub with a public build. Read the code, build it yourself, send a fix.

Transparent limitations

Windows only, 64-bit. Builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn once. Some newer menu texts are not translated into every language yet, help is welcome.

Built on JPEGView.

Lucid Viewer started as a fork of JPEGView and keeps its fast image pipeline at its core. Thanks to David Kleiner, who wrote JPEGView, to Kevin M (sylikc), who maintained it, to qbnu for the updated codecs, and to all JPEGView translators and contributors.
